Wi-Fi settings and help

Direct connection: hold the touch pad while powering DeskPal on, join the DeskPal-xxxx Wi-Fi shown on its OLED, and use http://192.168.4.1 with the token shown on the setup page. Your computer may say this network has no internet; that is expected. To use your router instead, enter http://deskpal.local or DeskPal’s router IP.

  1. Choose Connect to DeskPal above to start the live Wi-Fi link.
  2. While connected directly to DeskPal, change router Wi-Fi details on http://192.168.4.1. The dashboard’s scan button is for router-connected mode.
  3. Choose Save and restart DeskPal. Wait for it to reconnect, then update its address above if needed.

Room sensor calibration

Connect to calibrate

Compare the BME280 with a separate room thermometer after both sit in the same airflow for 10–15 minutes. Enter reference minus BME reading. The correction is saved on DeskPal and also affects its mood. Keep the BME280 away from the ESP32 and other warm parts; a software correction cannot remove changing heat from nearby components.
